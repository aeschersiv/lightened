The cover is split between the bands. We're a residential street, so the loud part wraps up on time.

The studio

A home studio with the setup to track a full band live and still keep every player on their own channel.

Live room
The whole band plays together in one room, and each instrument is captured separately so the mix stays flexible.
Drum room
Drums get their own room, which keeps them out of the other mics and sounds excellent on its own.
Natural reverb
The rooms are treated and tuned, so the space you hear on the record is the space you played in.
Gear for every era
Great mics, legendary gear, modern software, and a choice of amps. Bring your own if you'd rather.
Room to breathe
A living room inside and a place to relax outside between takes.
Coming from out of town
We have rooms for visiting artists. Book them ahead and we'll send you the Airbnb link.

Your engineer is James Tuttle

“I'll give you my suggestions, but the call is yours. My job is to capture your art the way you want it captured.”

James has spent ten years mixing and producing records. He trained with Eddie Kramer, the engineer behind Jimi Hendrix, Led Zeppelin and David Bowie, and he brings that rock and roll ear to every session.

He's also a multi-instrumentalist. If your song needs a part nobody in the band plays, he can probably play it. He studied music performance and audio engineering at Western Washington University.

Has worked with Cosmic Peace, Dave Kerly, Ghouls in the Basement, Jester King and others.

Best at rock and roll, psych rock, indie rock, indie pop, punk, new wave and shoegaze. Happy to work in any genre.
